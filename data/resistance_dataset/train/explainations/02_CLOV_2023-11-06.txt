The 1.22 level is a key resistance level that has been tested multiple times. It is a round number and often acts as a psychological barrier.
The $1.22 to to $1.41 is a zone to avoid since the price bounces off these levels repeatedly, both prices act as support or resistance depending
on what the current price is. All time high in the chart also acts as resistance. Levels before the all time 
high are discarded since the all time highs (on the chart) supercede them.
